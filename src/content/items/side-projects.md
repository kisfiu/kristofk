## First Day at the office (game)

A small top-down browser game I made for onboarding at TOPdesk: your first day as a developer at the Budapest office. Get your badge, ride the lift, survive the HR quiz, pick a laptop and find the DevEx team before lunch. About five minutes. Plain JavaScript, pixel art, runs in any browser and on phones.

## tvtime

My personal watch log: a small Flask app that records the films and shows I watch, enriches them from TMDb, and adds stats, a "what to watch next" page, mood-based recommendations and a Month/Year in review.

## This site

Astro + TypeScript, XP.css, View Transitions and the Popover API, tested with Playwright and Vitest, deployed by GitHub Actions.
