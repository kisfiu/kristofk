**Founder, board member · 2016–present**

A mentoring program for children living in temporary homes. Every child gets a volunteer mentor who meets them weekly to study, prepare for tests and talk through challenges, from kindergarten to the final exam. So no child is left behind because of money.

- **83** children and about **80** volunteers today, in **3** cities
- **400+** children helped over the years
- I write and manage projects with the board, handle finances and partner institutions, and have run the Budapest branch since 2021

It started as a small project with three friends in our hometown, Debrecen.
